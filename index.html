Cosuki — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 September 2026

The short version: Cosuki collects nothing about you. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and there is no Cosuki server for your data to be sent to. What you do in the app is saved on your device, and a copy is kept in your own Apple or Google account so that reinstalling the app does not cost you your progress. We cannot see that copy, and neither can anyone else.

What Cosuki saves

All of it is created by you, on your device, and none of it identifies you unless you choose a name that does:

  • The display name you choose — a nickname is fine, Cosuki never asks for your real name — and the avatar you pick.
  • Your learning progress: XP, which lessons and chapters you have finished, your day streak, the dates you were active, hearts, and any items you have earned.
  • Your settings: language, light or dark theme, learning mode, sound, and whether the daily reminder is on and at what time.
  • Whether you have bought the Cosuki Plus unlock.

Where it is kept

Two places, both yours:

  • Your device, in Cosuki's private app storage.
  • Your own Apple or Google account, as a backup — the iCloud key-value store on iPhone and iPad, and Android's own backup service on Android. This is what lets your progress come back if you delete the app and install it again, or move to a new phone.

That backup is stored under your account, not ours. Cosuki has no server of any kind, so there is nowhere for your data to be sent to us even in principle. On iPhone and iPad the backup only exists if you are signed in to iCloud; if you are not, Cosuki simply keeps everything on the device and nothing is copied anywhere.

When Cosuki uses the network

The app works offline. It reaches the network for exactly two things, and neither of them involves us:

  • The App Store or Google Play, to ask the price of the Cosuki Plus unlock and whether you have bought it.
  • Your own iCloud or Google account, to keep the backup described above up to date.

What Cosuki does not do

  • No user accounts, no sign-up, no email address, no password.
  • No analytics, crash reporting, or advertising software of any kind.
  • No tracking, no advertising identifiers, no profiling.
  • No access to your location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone.
  • No data is sold, shared, or disclosed to anyone, because none is collected.

Reminders

If you switch on the daily lesson reminder, the notification is scheduled by your own device and shown by your own device, at the time you choose. Nothing is sent to a server, and the reminder text contains no personal information. You can turn reminders off at any time in Settings, or in your device's notification settings.

Purchases

If you buy the Cosuki Plus unlock, the purchase is handled entirely by Apple or Google. They process the payment and tell the app only whether the unlock has been bought. Cosuki never sees or stores your name, card details, or billing address. Their handling of that information is covered by Apple's and Google's own privacy policies.

Because the purchase is recorded against your Apple or Google account rather than by us, you can restore it on a new device with Restore purchases in Settings.

Children

Cosuki is a maths learning app and is suitable for all ages. It collects no personal information about anyone, child or adult: nothing is gathered by us, nothing is profiled, and nothing is shared. The backup described above is held in the account of whoever owns the device, under that account's own protections and controls.

Deleting your data

You are always in control of everything Cosuki has saved:

  • Open Settings → Privacy & local data → Delete my data to erase your name, avatar, progress, XP, streak, items and settings. This clears the backup copy as well as the one on your device. It is immediate and cannot be undone.
  • Uninstalling the app is not the same thing. It removes what is on the device, but the backup in your own Apple or Google account remains, which is exactly what makes reinstalling painless. If you want everything gone, use Delete my data before you uninstall.

Because we hold nothing ourselves, there is no request to send us and nothing for us to delete on your behalf.

Changes to this policy

This policy is updated before a change ships, not after. Its previous version said that nothing left your device at all; that stopped being true when Cosuki began keeping a backup in your own account so that reinstalling would not cost you your progress, and this version was published with that change.

If Cosuki ever begins collecting information — anything at all that reaches us rather than staying with you and your platform account — this policy will say so before that feature ships.
